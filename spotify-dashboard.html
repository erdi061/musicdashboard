setup — just once

This runs entirely in your browser — no server, no database. Your listening data goes straight from Spotify to you. Four short steps.

  • 1Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in with your normal Spotify account — no special developer account needed.
  • 2Click Create app. Give it any name/description. For Redirect URI, paste the exact URL you're viewing this page at right now (shown below) — it has to match exactly, including http/https.
  • 3In the app's settings, tick the Web API checkbox, save, then copy the Client ID shown right on the app's overview page.
  • 4Paste that Client ID below and connect. You'll be sent to Spotify to approve access, then land back here.
